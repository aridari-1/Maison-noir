About Maison Noir

A ritual, dressed as an object.

Maison Noir started from a simple observation: most quitting tools ask you to track, log, and quantify your way out of a habit. We wanted something that felt like part of the habit itself something you reach for, the way you'd reach for the real thing.

The Idea

Light this instead.

Every piece in the collection is a signed digital object a case, a piece, a moment. When you'd normally reach for a cigarette, you open it instead. You light it. It smokes, briefly, the way the real thing would. Then it's gone, permanently, the same way a real cigarette is gone once it's smoked.

To do it again, you choose another piece. That's not a limitation it's the whole point. The friction of choosing again is the ritual. It gives you a beat to actually decide, rather than reaching on autopilot.

Signed, Not Speculative

Why this isn't an NFT.

Early on, this looked like it might become an NFT collection. We moved away from that on purpose. An NFT implies a market resale, speculation, value going up or down. None of that belongs here.

Instead, each piece is a plain digital object with a cryptographic signature attached the same category of technology banks and governments use to prove a document is authentic, adapted into something anyone can verify in their browser, instantly, without a blockchain, a wallet, or a gas fee. You can check any certificate's authenticity yourself, right now, on the verify page.

Honesty About Limits

What this is not.

  • —Not a medical device, a cessation program, or a substitute for professional treatment. If you're trying to quit smoking, a doctor or a licensed program can offer things we can't: nicotine replacement guidance, medication, structured support.
  • —Not a tracker with any verification behind it. Nothing here can confirm whether you actually skipped a real cigarette. That honesty is intentional this is a commitment ritual, not a surveillance tool.
  • —Not an investment. Prices are fixed per tier, supply is unlimited by design, and nothing about owning a piece is meant to appreciate in value.
  • —Not a guarantee of any outcome. Some people find a ritual like this genuinely useful for building awareness around a habit. Others won't. We'd rather say that plainly than oversell it.
On Availability

No scarcity, on purpose.

You'll notice there's no countdown, no "only 12 left," no urgency. Every tier is available without limit. If this is genuinely useful to you as a daily ritual, you shouldn't be turned away by a cap someone set for marketing reasons. The four tiers exist for taste and craftsmanship, not exclusivity.
